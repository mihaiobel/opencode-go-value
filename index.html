Methodology

Role matrix

  • Orchestrator — largest req/5h among models with AA intel ≥ 38, or the muse-spark special case (no_data but the largest quota). Many small turns, cache discount counts.
  • Dev — maximises AA speed × req/5h at AA intel ≥ 34 and a monthly limit ≥ $30. Frequency beats peak.
  • Reviewer — highest AA intel at req/5h ≥ 1000 in a model family that neither Dev nor Researcher occupies (uncorrelated failures).
  • Researcher — cheapest AA $/task at req/5h ≥ 3000, usable intelligence (≥ 34), and not the Dev model.

Free exclusion

Free models can never be recommended:

  • Limited time: their continuation is not guaranteed — no stable best-value recommendation is possible.
  • Timeouts / unstable: free tiers are throttled under load (rate limits, 429s/timeouts).
  • No dedicated AA slugs: no reliable benchmark dataset (404). Where mapped, the origin is flagged (e.g. step-5).

Families are derived from the id prefix (e.g. deepseek-*, mimo-*, claude-*). Missing values are never guessed.

Daily prompt

For other opencode setups: copy and run daily to re-evaluate.

Re-evaluate the opencode Go models ($10 plan, NOT Go Plus).

Sources:
- https://opencode.ai/docs/go/  (Go column of the pricing and requests tables)
- https://opencode.ai/inference/go/v1/models  (model ids)
- https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/<slug>  (intel index, $/task, speed)

Procedure:
1. For every Go model collect req/5h, monthly limit, in/out/cached prices and AA metrics.
   If the AA page is missing: mark it "no AA data", do not invent anything.
2. Apply the role matrix:
   - orchestrator: largest req/5h at AA intel >= 38 (or the muse-spark special case: largest quota, no_data).
   - dev: max(AA speed × req/5h) at AA intel >= 34 and monthly limit >= $30.
   - reviewer: highest AA intel at req/5h >= 1000 in a family other than dev/researcher.
   - researcher: lowest AA $/task at req/5h >= 3000, AA intel >= 34, not the dev model.
3. Exclude free models (longcat-2.5-preview-free, step-5-preview-free): limited time, unstable, no dedicated AA slug.
4. Output: recommendation table (role | model id | rationale | key numbers) plus the full model table.
