Methodology

Role matrix

  • Orchestrator: largest req/5h among models with AA intel ≥ 38.
  • Dev: maximises AA speed × req/5h at AA intel ≥ 34 and a monthly limit ≥ $30.
  • Reviewer: highest AA intel at req/5h ≥ 1000 in a family that neither Dev nor Researcher occupies.
  • Researcher: cheapest AA $/task at req/5h ≥ 3000, usable intelligence of 34 or more, and not the Dev model.

Families come from the id prefix: the leading letters of a model id. Missing values are never guessed.

Combined score

A second source, LMArena Overall as of 2026-10-08, feeds a combined score. The score is the mean of the normalised values from both sources, and mapped or missing Arena entries are marked in the table.

Free exclusion

Free models can never be recommended:

  • Limited time: their continuation is not guaranteed, so no stable recommendation is possible.
  • Unstable: free tiers throttle under load and hit 429s and timeouts.
  • No dedicated AA slug: no reliable benchmark data, 404.

Daily prompt

For other opencode setups: copy and run daily to re-evaluate.

Re-evaluate the opencode Go models, $10 plan, NOT Go Plus.

Sources:
- https://opencode.ai/docs/go/  Go column of the pricing and requests tables
- https://opencode.ai/inference/go/v1/models  model ids
- https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/<slug>  intel index, $/task, speed

Procedure:
1. For every Go model collect req/5h, monthly limit, in/out/cached prices and AA metrics.
   If the AA page is missing: mark it "no AA data", do not invent anything.
2. Apply the role matrix:
   - orchestrator: largest req/5h at AA intel >= 38.
   - dev: max of AA speed times req/5h at AA intel >= 34 and monthly limit >= $30.
   - reviewer: highest AA intel at req/5h >= 1000 in a family other than dev or researcher.
   - researcher: lowest AA $/task at req/5h >= 3000, AA intel >= 34, and not the dev model.
3. Exclude models flagged as free: limited time, unstable, no dedicated AA slug.
4. Output: recommendation table with role, model id, rationale and key numbers, plus the full model table.
