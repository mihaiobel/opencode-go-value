Methodology

Role matrix

  • Orchestrator: largest req/5h among models with AA intel ≥ 38.
  • Dev: maximises AA speed times req/5h, at AA intel ≥ 34 and a monthly limit of at least $30.
  • Reviewer: highest AA intel at req/5h ≥ 1000, in a family no other role uses.
  • Researcher: cheapest AA $/task at req/5h ≥ 3000 and AA intel ≥ 34, and never the Dev model.

A family is the letter prefix of a model id. Missing values never get guessed.

Combined score

The Score column blends both benchmark sources. Each source is scaled to 0 to 100, then the two values are averaged. Mapped or missing Arena values are marked in the table.

Why free models are out

Free models never get picked. Three reasons:

  • Limited time. They can vanish any day, so no pick would hold.
  • Unstable. Free tiers throttle under load and throw 429s.
  • No own AA page. There is no benchmark data to check.

Daily prompt

Works in any opencode setup. Copy it, run it once a day, get fresh picks.

Re-evaluate the opencode Go models, $10 plan, NOT Go Plus.

Sources:
- https://opencode.ai/docs/go/  Go column of the pricing and requests tables
- https://opencode.ai/inference/go/v1/models  model ids
- https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/<slug>  intel index, $/task, speed

Procedure:
1. For every Go model collect req/5h, monthly limit, in/out/cached prices and AA metrics.
   If the AA page is missing: mark it "no AA data", do not invent anything.
2. Apply the role matrix:
   - orchestrator: largest req/5h at AA intel >= 38.
   - dev: max of AA speed times req/5h at AA intel >= 34 and monthly limit >= $30.
   - reviewer: highest AA intel at req/5h >= 1000 in a family other than dev or researcher.
   - researcher: lowest AA $/task at req/5h >= 3000, AA intel >= 34, and not the dev model.
3. Exclude models flagged as free: limited time, unstable, no dedicated AA slug.
4. Output: recommendation table with role, model id, rationale and key numbers, plus the full model table.
