Methodology and notes

Role matrix

  • Orchestrator: largest req/5h among models with AA intel at least 38.
  • Dev: highest AA speed times req/5h, at AA intel at least 34 and a monthly limit of at least $30.
  • Reviewer: highest AA intel at req/5h at least 1000, in a family no other role uses.
  • Researcher: cheapest AA cost per task at req/5h at least 3000 and AA intel at least 34, never the dev model.

Score

The Score column blends both benchmark sources. Each source is scaled to 0 to 100 between the lowest and the highest value among the listed models, so both weigh the same. The scaled values are then averaged. A missing source is skipped, never guessed.

Notes

A family is the letter prefix of a model id. req/5h is the estimate from the docs, not a hard limit. Where a model has price tiers, the base tier counts.

Re-evaluate the opencode Go models, $10 plan, NOT Go Plus, and recommend the best
model per agent role.

Sources, primary only, never guess:
- https://opencode.ai/docs/go/  -> the Go column of the pricing and requests tables
  only, not Go Plus. Read req/5h, monthly limit in USD, in/out/cached prices in $/1M.
- https://opencode.ai/inference/go/v1/models  -> full model ids.
- https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/<slug>  -> Intelligence Index, Cost per Task in USD,
  output speed in t/s. If the AA page is missing with a 404, mark it "no AA data", do not guess.
- https://lmarena.ai/leaderboard/text  -> LMArena Overall score, latest ranking. If the
  entry is missing, mark it "missing" and do not guess.

Role matrix:
- orchestrator: largest req/5h among models with AA intel >= 38.
- dev: maximise AA speed times req/5h at AA intel >= 34 and monthly limit >= $30.
- reviewer: highest AA intel at req/5h >= 1000 in a model family not used by
  orchestrator, dev or researcher, where a family is the leading letters of the model id.
- researcher: lowest AA $/Task at req/5h >= 3000, AA intel >= 34, and not the dev model.

Models flagged as free are excluded: limited-time only, unstable and
timeout-prone, no own AA slug.

Score: compute a combined score per model as the mean of the scaled AA and
LMArena values. Scale each source to 0 to 100 between the lowest and highest
value among the listed models. Mark mapped and missing Arena values in the table.

Output:
1. Role table: role | model id | rationale | key numbers, meaning req/5h, $/month, AA intel, AA speed, AA $/Task.
2. Full model table with every Go model; mark free models as "excluded".
3. Call out missing AA data explicitly as "no AA data".
